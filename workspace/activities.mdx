**Activities** models operational habits/activities (beyond a one-off task): streaks, per-day logs, reminders.

## What you can do

- **Assign** the activity to one or more studio members (email reminders respect that list)
- Define an activity with schedule and recurrence (daily / weekly / monthly / custom), including slots per weekday
- Choose the **type** on create (habit or time block); after it is created it cannot be changed
- **Habits:** mark complete / cancel the day (with optional reason), streaks and completion rate derived from their logs. Habits can be shared (`members` \| `followers` \| `public`). Public URL: `/@handle/activities/:id` (name, description, image, recurrence — no logs, streaks, assignees, or reminders).
- **Time blocks:** agenda/timeline only (sleep, meals, transport…); no manual marking and no streak. Today shows a block as complete automatically after its end time, without creating a log
- **Pause recurrence** without deleting the activity: it stops counting in the **Today** view (agenda, ribbon, day streaks); it stays visible in **grid** and **list** so you can resume or edit it
- **Reset history** (habits, detail ⋯ menu): deletes logs, streaks, and completion rate and resets `createdAt` so the habit behaves as newly created, without losing schedule or settings
- On the **detail**, a header with image, title, type, **Log today**, and a properties rail (repeat, schedule, assignees, tags, pause, and email). Activities are **not** linked to a project. Three summary cards on habits (streak, month completion, and sessions) and a monthly calendar. The calendar marks and counts only configured recurrence weekdays; unscheduled days stay empty. The **Scheduled** block (Mon–Sun) also shows on daily habits. Habits are marked with **Log today** or by tapping a day; time blocks only show the schedule, with no done/not-done and no streak cards
- Active scheduled occurrences appear in the studio [Calendar](/workspace/calendar) automatically. Email reminders with an offset remain optional
- Attach images (manual upload or **AI generation** from the card: pre-filled prompt, preview, confirm → Vercel Blob / `activity_images`)
- Export the activities list and each activity's logs to CSV from their bars.
- On the detail **tags** rail, **Organize activity with AI** can add up to three existing tags and two goal links from the title and description (Undo restores links only). In the recurrence editor, **Set recurrence with AI** picks an explicit daily/weekly/monthly/custom schedule when the description states one; ambiguous text leaves recurrence unchanged.

## Insights

The **Insights** tab (`/activities/insights`) shows active/paused habits, blocks, weekly adherence, streaks, and daily load. Paused blocks and habits do not enter adherence denominators. API: `GET /api/activities/profiles/:studioId/insights-summary?todayLocalDateKey=YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Routes

| Route | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `/activities` | Today / grid / list (`?view=`). In Today view the date control goes first (as on Today); `?date=YYYY-MM-DD` shows habits and blocks for that day (with no param it is civil today) |
| `/activities/insights` | Insights (adherence and load) |
| `/activities/:activityId` | Detail |

Plan limit: activities per studio.

## Follow-ups

- **Quantity / weekly frequency goals** (e.g. “3× per week” or a counter per period) are not modeled: streak and completion rate assume a binary log per period. Adding them would require a new schema + streak rules; it is out of this scope.
