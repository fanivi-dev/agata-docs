Calendar's own items are **reminders**. They can be:

- **One day** or **several days** (inclusive start and end range).
- With an **agenda time**, or marked **all day**.
- Optionally **annual** (only if they are one day).

In a multi-day range the reminder appears on each day of the calendar.

<Note>
  For a task that should remain visible inside Agata until you resolve it, use a task **must-do** instead. Must-dos are persistent studio UI; reminders are calendar items and optional email delivery. See [Tasks](/workspace/tasks#must-dos).
</Note>

## Create and edit

- From Calendar (toolbar **Add Reminder**, click on a week/day gap, or the day panel).
- A single form: title, description, dates, all day / time, assignment, and email options.
- Edit or delete: open the item detail → **Edit** / **Delete** (also from the day sidebar).

## Assignment and email

- **Assigned to** indicates who receives the reminder by email (if email is enabled).
- If there are no assignees, the email goes to the **creator**.
- Email is optional: choose when to notify (week / 3 days / previous day / same day) and the send time.
- By default the email is anchored to the **first day** of the range.
- On multi-day ranges you can enable **Email each day** to repeat the same relative notice for each day of the range.

## Tips

- Export reminders to CSV from the calendar bar.
- A range can span at most 366 days.
- Only **one-shot** single-day reminders (not annual) can be **rescheduled by dragging** in week or day view.
- All-day reminders (and other untimed items) live in the **All day** row of week/day.
- Timezone matters: keep local time consistent.
