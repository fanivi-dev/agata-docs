Agata is a **personal assistant** that turns what you want into a plan and supported next steps. It is grounded in a **studio** of day-to-day and work tools. Studios can also **share** Nutrition recipes and meal plans with people who are not members.

## In one sentence

> A personal assistant that turns goals into next steps, grounded in one studio of specialized apps, with a home in **Today**.

## Who it is for

| Mode | Who | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Personal** | One person | Full life + productivity; optional public Nutrition |
| **Members / brand** | Studio with members | Collaboration on notebook, tasks, projects; share lists without adding every reader |

## The three layers you will see in the app

```txt
Studio  →  public actor + members + billing (`profileId`)
     └── Studio apps  →  tasks, finance, health…
     └── Share     →  /@handle recipes, plans, library, collections, goals, meals
```

Detail: [Product model](/concepts/product-model) and [Studios, members, and follow](/concepts/organizations-studios).

## Product surfaces

### Daily

- **Today** — studio home ([detail](/workspace/today)): date control, next action, capture, day line, and control rail.
- **Notebook** — rich-text writing and groups ([detail](/workspace/notebook)).
- **Week plan** — Mon–Sun planning for Daily.

### Work

- **Tasks** — task inbox (it is no longer the landing).
- **Projects, tags, activities, calendar, reminders** — organize and plan over time.

### Apps

- [Goals](/apps/goals) · [Finance](/apps/finance) · [Library](/apps/library)  
- [Nutrition](/apps/nutrition) · [Health](/apps/health) · [Pomodoro](/apps/pomodoro)

### Platform

- Accounts and sign-in  
- [Plans and billing](/platform/billing-plans)  
- [Connections](/platform/connections) (people network), [Explore](/platform/explore) (recommended public studios), audit logs (admins), internal feedback  

## How navigation feels

- Unified sidebar + **app launcher**
- **⌘K** to jump between areas
- Each app can tint the UI with its **theme** (see the design system)

Short guide: [Navigation and shell](/getting-started/navigation).

## Quick glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
