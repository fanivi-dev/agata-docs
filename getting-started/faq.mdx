## Where do I land when I enter a studio?

In **Today** (`/studios/:id/today`). That is the canonical home. The Tasks inbox lives at `/tasks`.

## Where do I write daily notes?

In **Notebook** (`/notebook`): note inbox, groups, and rich-text editor. See [Notebook](/workspace/notebook).

## Can I turn Tasks off?

No. `tasks-app` is **always-on**.

## Why don't I see Finance / Health in a team studio?

In team those apps are usually **disabled** until an admin enables them. Tasks, projects, tags, library, and notebook are usually on.

## What happens if I hit a plan limit?

Member capacity and AI credits depend on the studio plan and its extras. Tasks, projects, tags, reminders, and other studio content are unlimited. Detail: [Plans](/platform/billing-plans).

## Can I downgrade without deleting studio content?

Yes. The downgrade is scheduled for the **end of the period**, and studio content remains available. The lower plan changes member capacity, monthly AI credits, and the Agent image pool.

## Are there multiple languages?

Yes: **English** and **Spanish**. See [Locale](/concepts/locale-and-i18n).

## Is the notes editor the same as the tasks editor?

Same rich-text stack; the **body** is stored as a versioned document separate from metadata. In the product: you write in the rich-text editor; you do not need the HTTP detail unless you are developing.

## Who can see audit logs?

Studio owners and admins. Route `/logs`.

## What if I open a URL that does not exist?

Agata shows a **404** page with a link home. Studio and other product routes still ask you to sign in when you are signed out.

## Where do I give product feedback?

With the feedback modal in the user menu; the review hub is internal. See [Feedback](/platform/feedback).

## Where is the technical API / DB documentation?

**API** and **Database** tabs. Frontend/Backend are drafts on purpose.
