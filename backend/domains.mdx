Product domains under `internal/<context>/` follow the layout described in
[Layout and layers](/backend/layout): domain, CQRS application (or Application that
embeds a ports backend), inbound/outbound ports, and adapters. Full list
in `scripts/check-api-architecture.mjs` (`migratedContexts`).

## Seed

`internal/seed/` + `cmd/seed` provides a stable development dataset (work, life, finance, Studios, feedback…). By default it attaches the demo Studio **Agata Full Demo** to the local owner `seed@agata.local` / `SeedDemo123!` (Better Auth credential). It creates 3 connected demo users (`maya/jordan/sam.seed@agata.local`); Maya and Jordan join as members, Sam is a former member whose library rows remain. The seed also creates public Explore Studios, an Organization with Teams assigned to org-owned Studios, follows, pending invites, and share visibility on recipes, meal plans, meal days, library, and goals. Fixture counts are deliberately independent from commercial entitlements and private abuse safeguards. The seed invalidates the usage cache in Upstash and **does not overwrite** an existing Stripe plan. Re-running without `--reset` is idempotent.
