## Unified shell

A single chrome for every app in the profile:

- `shared/layout/unified-profile-shell.tsx`
- `shared/layout/profile-shell-frame.tsx`
- Header breadcrumbs: catalog-derived trail in `use-profile-header-breadcrumbs.ts`; extra crumbs via `profile-page-chrome-context`

Domain `*AppShell` files are thin wrappers (theme / slots).

### Sidebar (concept)

List without headers: Today, Week plan, Calendar, Tasks, Notebook, Activities; then domain apps; Projects/Tags at the end. Audit logs do not go in the sidebar.

### Active area

`shared/constants/profile-area-catalog.ts` is the data-only source for:

- area and segments after `/profiles/:id/`
- entitlement app (including the Tasks family)
- theme, icon, destination, group, and order
- i18n references for shell and command palette pages

`detectProfileAppArea`, the access filter, the shell, and the palette derive from that catalog. It contains no hooks, React elements, providers, or server-only imports.

Runtime capabilities stay explicit: domain tabs nest under sidebar destinations from `shared/constants/profile-area-catalog.ts`, requests in the quick-capture registry, and provider order in `persistent-profile-shell.tsx`.

### Per-app themes

`data-app-theme`: `today`, `weekplan`, `notebook`, `tasks`, `projects`, `tags`, `calendar`, `finance`, `health`, `library`, `nutrition`, `goals`, `pomodoro`, `agent`.

## Path helpers

Source of truth: `apps/app/shared/navigation/paths.ts`.

- `StudioPaths`, `FinancePaths`, `LibraryPaths`, `NutritionPaths`, `HealthPaths`, `NotebookPaths`, `PomodoroPaths`, `GoalsPaths`
- `getProfilePath`, `pathnameWithoutLocalePrefix` (legacy `/en`/`/es` only); product paths without a locale prefix

Do not hand-build `/profiles/${id}/finance` in features.

## Locale-aware navigation

From `apps/app/i18n/navigation.ts`:

- `Link`, `useRouter`, `usePathname`, `redirect`

From `next/navigation` when applicable: `useParams`, `useSearchParams`, `notFound`.

## Proxy

`apps/app/proxy.ts`:

- Protects known product prefixes (`isProtectedPath`: `/studios`, `/connections`, `/explore`, `/feedback`, `/account`, `/billing`, `/logs`, `/profile`, `/today`) with a Better Auth session
- Unknown page URLs are not gated — they render the root `app/not-found.tsx`
- Applies `next-intl`
- Does **not** rewrite locale on `/_workflow/*`
- **Bypasses** next-intl on `/api/*` (includes `/api/auth/*`)
