**Nutrition** covers the studio food cycle.

## Modules

| Module | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Meals** | Landing: what you ate, in a weekly grid or list |
| **Recipes** | Recipes and ingredients |
| **Inventory** | Pantry |
| **Shopping** | Shopping list |
| **Planner** | Weekly plan and meal slots |

## Meals and Planner: the week

Both screens open with the same **date control** as [Today](/workspace/today), Activities, and [Week plan](/apps/week-plan): arrows, **Today** button, Mon–Sun strip, and a calendar to jump to any day. In Meals the day goes in `?dateKey=`; in Planner, `?dateKey=` (any day of the week) or `?week=` (Monday).

Both screens share the same **7 days × 4 meals** grid (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack), with the snack row collapsed by default.

- **Day header** — day kcal and a bar for the protein / carbs / fat split (exact values on hover).
- **Chips** — title, servings, kcal, and avatars of who eats it. If there are no avatars, the general audience is implicit and no extra label is shown. In **Meals**, everything from today backward is marked *eaten*; later slots stay *planned*. In **Planner** everything is *planned* until you activate the plan.
- **Quick add** — the `+` in each cell opens suggestions based on what the household repeats most in that slot, plus saved recipes.
- **Metrics bar** — above the grid. In Meals: logged, daily kcal average, protein per day, and gaps. In Planner: planned slots out of 28, daily average, and repeats.

kcal and macros **are derived from the linked recipe × servings**. A meal with no recipe (or a recipe with no macros) shows `—` and does not count in averages. In the log panel, the macro summary only appears when at least one value is known and occupies its own row under servings.

The planner adds the plan lifecycle (**Draft → Active → Done**), the *Activate* button with the number of meals it will write, **Duplicate last week**, and **Repeat this slot** on several days from the side panel.

## Recipes: one place to edit

**The recipe page is the editor.** There is no `/edit` route: name, description, and servings are edited inline and **autosaved** (the header shows *Saving… / All changes saved*).

- **List** — recipe-card grid or compact list (toggle in the filter bar), with search over name, description, and tags, sort (recently updated / name / newest first), and *Show N more* pagination.
- **Tag filter** — chips with counts, AND mode (a recipe must carry every selected tag). They come from the studio tag graph (`domainScope: nutrition`, entity `nutrition_recipe`), not from a column on the recipe.
- **Create** — side panel with name, description, servings, and tags, then leaves you on the recipe page.
- **Detail** — cover (upload or generate with AI), inline fields, servings selector, ingredient / tool / step editors, and a collapsible side panel with people and studio context (projects).

### Ingredients

The ingredient block is an inline editor: each row has name, quantity, and unit, **saves on blur** (or with `Enter`), reorders with the arrows, and deletes with `×`. The bottom row adds a new one. Maximum **200 ingredients** per recipe.

Quantities are **absolute**: they describe the servings the recipe itself declares, so the servings selector **does not** rescale them — it changes the stored value and the block header (*for N servings*) reflects it.

Each ingredient row also has an optional **food category** (native selector, clearable) and **Set food category with AI** beside it. Recipe tags use **Add recipe tags with AI** on the recipe header (additive, up to three tags per run).

### AI classification

Field-local controls (with undo) cover:

- **Recipe tags** — additive tag suggestions on the recipe page.
- **Meal type** — exclusive classification when editing a meal log.
- **Food category** — exclusive classification on recipe ingredients, pantry items, and shopping items (same category list everywhere).

Manual category edits use the same selector; clearing removes the stored category.

### Ingredient replacement

Each ingredient row has **Replace ingredient with AI** (curated catalog, unit/ratio math in the API). Studio **Ingredient preferences** in the recipe overflow menu stores excluded catalog ingredients, allergens, and required diet labels; changing preferences invalidates in-flight substitution results. Undo restores the full prior row when its revision is unchanged.

### Tools

Utensil list (name only): same inline-edit pattern, reorder, and maximum **200** per recipe. They are not sent to shopping.

### Steps

Ordered instructions (multiline text): save on blur (or `⌘/Ctrl+Enter`), reorder with arrows, and maximum **200** per recipe.

### Recipe actions

- **Duplicate** — copies the recipe with ingredients, steps, and tools and the *(copy)* suffix, then opens the copy. The cover **is not** copied: blobs belong to the entity that uploaded them.
- **Send to shopping** — creates one shopping item per ingredient (`source: recipe`, linked to the recipe). Disabled while the recipe has no ingredients.
- **Print** and **Copy as markdown** — export name, description, servings, ingredients, tools, and steps as stored.

## Shopping: the list and store mode

Search with no matches has its own empty state and a clear-search action. Mine and Auto-added also distinguish an empty filtered view from a clear shopping list. The cart progress summarizes bought items; pantry shortfalls already on the list are described as covered by the list, not as replenished stock. Once a linked pantry item reaches its minimum, its provenance label shows the pantry quantity without claiming it is still below minimum.

The list is **grouped by provenance**, not by aisle: *Below minimum* (`source: low_stock`), *From recipes* (`source: recipe`), and *Added by hand* (`source: manual`). Each row shows its concrete origin under the name — the recipe that requested it, or the pantry `2 of 6 cup`.

- **Metrics** — to buy (with a progress bar of what is already bought), from recipes, below minimum, and assigned.
- **Filters** — `All`, `Mine`, `Auto-added` (recipe + pantry), and `Bought`, plus search over name, unit, and origin.
- **Right rail** — shopping-run summary and **suggestions**: pantry items below their minimum that are not yet on the list, with *Add all* (bulk create). The run can also **set a store** (name and date) on a shopping collection.
- **Detail** (`/nutrition/shopping/[itemId]`) — *Why it is on the list* links to the source recipe or pantry item; activity (created / bought / last change); quantity, status, and assignment. When the item comes from a pantry minimum, *Mark as bought* can **top the pantry up** in the same gesture. If the linked recipe was deleted, the row keeps the line with provenance *From a recipe that no longer exists*; **Keep as manual item** clears the recipe link and leaves a normal manual shopping line (`clearRecipeLink` on PATCH).
- **Store mode** (`/nutrition/shopping/store`) — single column, large rows, and group-by-group progress with a progress bar. Ticking here writes the same status as the list. Each visit starts with items that are still pending; purchases from previous visits
  are excluded from its cart counter. Rows remain visible after being checked so
  they can be unchecked during the visit. A completed visit shows a completion
  message. Each row stays disabled until its status write finishes.

## Inventory: the pantry

Items are grouped by **location** (`location`), with a count and how many need attention. Each row shows the stock bar against its minimum, the expiry pill, and the quantity stepper. Pantry `location` is a storage shelf such as Fridge or Cupboard.

- **Metrics** — active, expired, expiring (7 days), and below minimum (with *Send all to shopping*). The attention bar counts each item once, including items with no stock, even when it is both low and near expiry.
- **Use it up first** — strip of what expires in the next week.
- **Filters** — `All`, `Low stock`, `Expiring`, `Expired`, search, and location facet. A filtered view with no matches offers **Clear filters**, which resets search, scope, and location; the first-item prompt appears only when the pantry is empty.
- **To shopping** — creates the item with `source: low_stock` and `inventoryItemId`, for the amount missing to the minimum. If one is already pending, the row shows *On the list* instead of the button.
- **Detail** (`/nutrition/inventory/[itemId]`) — stock bar with the minimum mark, expiry, linked shopping items, notes, quantity stepper, and edit in a side panel.

Pantry create and edit panels identify invalid fields next to their controls.
Quantity and restock actions show pending state and prevent overlapping writes.
Deleting a meal plan requires confirmation naming the week and number of slots.

## Collaboration

Team content is visible to studio members. Recipes and meal plans can also
be **shared** without membership:

| Visibility | Who can see |
| --- | --- |
| Team only (`members`) | Members with the nutrition section (default) |
| Followers | Team, or a signed-in follower of the studio |
| Anyone with the link (`public`) | Signed-out included |

Public URLs: `/@handle/recipes/:recipeId`, `/@handle/plans/:planId`, and
`/@handle/meals/:dateKey` (visible logs that day: title, photo, meal
type, optional visible recipe). The public studio catalog lists
recipes, meal plans, and recent meal days the viewer may see.
Inventory and shopping stay members-only. Public meal JSON has no
macros, notes, or assignees.

Collaboration inside the team uses the same pattern as tasks:

- **`createdBy`** — who created the item  
- **`assignedTo`** — one or more relevant people (meal/slot: who it is for; shopping: who buys; recipe/pantry: optional owners)

Empty `assignedTo` = general audience, no visible label. The **All** filter shows the whole studio; filtering by member shows items where that person is in `assignedTo`.

## Insights

The **Insights** tab (`/nutrition/insights`) summarizes log consistency (date window), macros only when known, mix by meal, planner occupancy, low/expired inventory, and pending shopping. API: `GET /api/nutrition/profiles/:studioId/insights-summary?startDateKey=&endDateKey=&todayLocalDateKey=`.

## Tips

- The meal planner (`/nutrition/planner`) **is not** the Daily [Week plan](/apps/week-plan) (`/week-plan`).  
- The planner can **materialize** meals into the log (copies slot assignees).  
- Shopping items can originate from recipes or inventory.  
- Meals downloads the visible week as CSV; Recipes, Inventory, and Shopping download the full studio dataset.
- The recipe cover is **added / replaced from detail** (manual upload or **AI generation**: prompt, preview, confirm → Vercel Blob / `imageUrl`). List cards only display it.  
- In team, often **opt-in**.
