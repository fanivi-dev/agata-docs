## Quick product map

| Area | What it includes | Typical routes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily** | Today (home), Notebook, Week plan | `/today`, `/notebook`, `/week-plan` |
| **Work** | Tasks (inbox), projects, tags, activities, calendar | `/tasks`, `/projects`, `/calendar` |
| **Apps** | Goals, finance, library, nutrition, health, pomodoro | `/goals`, `/finance`, … |
| **Platform** | Auth, billing, connections, logs, feedback | `/billing`, `/connections`, `/logs` |

## Product principles

- **One studio, many apps** — same context, shells with their own theme.
- **Today is home** — the studio landing is `/today`; Tasks is the inbox.
- **Personal and team** — personal org or team; in team, finance/health/nutrition can be opt-in.
- **i18n** — English and Spanish.
