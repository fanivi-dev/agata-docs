A **Studio** is the social object: content, members, followers, and
subscribers. A person can own a Studio, or an **Organization** can own
people and zero or more Studios. The former product name was
**workspace** (`workspaceId`).

## Studio

Public actor with a unique **name**. The **handle** is that name as a
slug (`Agata Full Demo` → `@agata_full_demo`) and stays in lockstep when
the name changes. A taken or reserved name is rejected; Agata does not
append `_2`. Reserved slugs include `inbox`, `membership`, and
`subscribe`. Studio apps live at `/orgs/:orgHandle/:handle/...`.
Personal uses `personal` as the org segment. If the organization has no
valid handle, the URL uses its id — the same fallback as a studio without
a handle. Shared Nutrition
and other public rows live at `/@handle` (RSC `/p/[handle]`; public API
`/api/p/:handle`).

Studios are **private** by default. A private studio’s `/@handle`
catalog is **404** for anyone who is not a member (including followers,
subscribers, and anonymous visitors). Members still see it. Set
**Public** in studio settings to publish the catalog. Shared rows still
honor their own visibility: a `public`, `followers`, or `subscribers`
item stays reachable at `/@handle/{kind}/:id` even when the catalog is
unpublished — the viewer must still pass that row’s audience. Follow and
Join still require a public studio.

| Kind | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Person** | Personal Studio. `organization_id` is null. One user owner. |
| **Organization** | Decorative when the Studio is org-owned (`organization_id` set). Not a parent org. |

**Organization** is a separate table (`organizations` + `organization_members`).
It is not a Studio and has no followers, subscribers, or public page.
The org **handle** is the name as a slug, same rules as a studio handle,
and it is always in product URLs. Billing stays on the Studio row. Existing `kind=organization` rows
with a null `organization_id` stay Studios.

Org admins create Studios under the Organization. Other org people do
not automatically become Studio members. Add people from the org directory
(**Manage organization → People**) at `/orgs/:orgHandle`, from studio
**Invite members** / `/orgs/:orgHandle/:handle/team`, or from
**Connections**. Transfer ownership (Studio settings, owner only) moves a
personal Studio to another member or into an Organization. Blocked while
an Agata SaaS subscription is active, and blocked while any fan
subscription is `active`, `trialing`, `past_due`, or `unpaid`. Org-owned
Studios cannot transfer in this version.

**Team** belongs to an Organization. It is a permission shortcut, not a
Studio: no handle, no followers, no Explore page. Assign a Team to an
org-owned Studio to upsert `profile_members` for those people. Unassign
removes membership that exists only via that Team; a direct invite stays.
One Studio can have several Teams; one Team can assign to several Studios.

## Organization navigation

Opening `/orgs` returns you to the last organization directory you visited,
including Personal. This selection is stored in your account preferences and
is checked against your current organization membership before redirecting.
If no saved selection is available, the organization picker appears; accounts
with only Personal go straight to `/orgs/personal`.

Use **All organizations** in the sidebar switcher to open `/orgs?view=all`
without the automatic redirect. The picker marks the last visited space and
supports search, cards, and list views. Organization directories use
`/orgs/:orgHandle`, falling back to the organization id when needed.

The directory separates **Studios** and **Trash**, with search and a removable
Team filter. Selecting a Team shows only its assigned Studios. Organization
owners and admins create Studios and open **Manage organization** for People,
Teams, and Settings. Members see guidance to request access when their list is
empty.

Organizations, Explore, Following, Connections, and Inbox share the product
sidebar's compact geometry, collapse controls, and saved open state. Studios
remains available when visiting another platform page. The desktop sidebar
also supports `Cmd+B` / `Ctrl+B`; narrow layouts use the navigation drawer.

## Members

People invited by email who can **run** the studio. Invite from the
studio switcher (**Invite members**) or `/orgs/:orgHandle/:handle/team`. That
route is the invite screen, not a Team product. Studio settings (name,
visibility, members, usage) live at `/orgs/:orgHandle/:handle/settings`, also
from the switcher. The `/studios` list does not open settings.

Non-owners can **leave** from Studio settings. The last owner cannot
leave (delete the studio instead). Leave does not unfollow and does not
cancel a paid membership to the same studio.

| Role | Can (indicative) |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything + ownership |
| **Admin** | Manage members, logs, sensitive config, opt-in apps |
| **Member** | Daily use of enabled apps |

Section access (`section_access`) is unchanged. The owner can always delete.

Invitations still reserve a **seat**. Followers and subscribers do not.

## Follow

One-way, free, no accept. A follower can see shareable rows set to
`followers` or `public`. Follow does not create a `profile_members` row
and does not count toward `seat_count`.

**Follow ≠ member.** Share a recipe or a library list without adding
someone as a member.

Unfollow does not cancel a paid membership.

## Subscribers

Paid audience. A subscriber is never a `profile_members` row and never
a seat. Product: [Memberships](/platform/memberships).

Access ladder: **team > subscriber > follower > public**. A subscriber
sees `subscribers` + `followers` + `public` without needing a follow
row. Subscribing **upserts a follow**. The owner cannot subscribe to
their own studio.

On `/@handle`, Join appears when the public studio returns active
plans. A signed-in subscriber sees Manage (buyer portal) and, when the
plan includes `owner_chat`, Message. A miss on a `subscribers` URL is
**404** (no existence leak). The catalog may hint that subscriber-only
items stay hidden; it does not list those titles.

Followed and entitled subscribed studios are listed at `/following`
(`GET /users/me/following`). The list includes active fan subscriptions even
when the studio is private or the follow row is missing; canceled subscriptions
drop off.

## Shareable rows

One audience enum on the row: `members` (default) \| `subscribers` \|
`followers` \| `public`. Member access for a miss is **section-scoped**
(nutrition, library, or goals). Policy lives in `internal/share` and
`shared/constants/share-visibility.ts`. Public URLs are
`/@handle/{kind}/:id`. Miss is **404**. Copy-link is available when
visibility is `public`, `followers`, or `subscribers`.

| Kind | Path |
| --- | --- |
| Recipe | `/@handle/recipes/:id` |
| Meal plan | `/@handle/plans/:id` |
| Library item | `/@handle/library/:id` |
| Library collection | `/@handle/collections/:id` |
| Goal | `/@handle/goals/:id` |
| Daily meals | `/@handle/meals/:dateKey` |
| Published note (`blog`, `research`, `report`) | `/@handle/notes/:id` |
| Exercise routine | `/@handle/routines/:id` |
| Vision board | `/@handle/boards/:id` |
| Habit | `/@handle/activities/:id` |

Finance, health metrics and sessions, Eve, inventory, shopping, tasks,
and unpublished notes stay members-only. A public parent never elevates
a `members` child. Public notes require type `blog` / `research` /
`report` and status `published`. Public activities are habits only (no
logs, streaks, or assignees). Public routines omit sessions and
attachments. Public vision boards list only goals the viewer may see.

## Organization lifecycle

An **Organization** has its own handle (name slug, same rules as a studio)
for `/orgs/:orgHandle` URLs. Owners rename or change the handle in org
settings. Org invitations (`admin` or `member`) accept or reject from the
invitee inbox; cancel from org people. **Leave** removes org membership;
**transfer ownership** moves the sole owner role. **Soft-delete** requires
zero org-owned studios and no pending org invitations; **restore** and
**permanent delete** follow the same trash pattern as studios (handle stays
reserved until permanent delete).

## Trash and multiple studios

Deleting a studio still moves it to trash for 30 days. `/@handle` is 404
while `deleted_at` is set. Follow rows stay so restore is lossless.
Permanent delete drops `profile_follows` for that studio. You can belong
to several. Pick an **Organization** at `/orgs` (or skip straight
to your only scope after sign-in). Each org’s studio list lives at
`/orgs/:orgHandle` (`personal` for person-owned studios; the
id if the name cannot slug). Outside a
Studio, the platform sidebar switcher changes scope or opens
`/orgs`. **Studios** appears after a scope is selected and opens
that org’s list. Inside a Studio the sidebar picker lists only that
org’s studios.
Trash and team filters use `?section=trash` and `?team=` on that route.
Legacy `/studios` redirects into the matching org directory.

Finding someone else's published handle lives on
[Explore](/platform/explore). Type `@handle` or a name that slugs to one.
It uses the same `GET /api/p/:handle` path as `/@handle`. There is no
public directory. Private studios stay 404. `/studios?section=public`
redirects there.
