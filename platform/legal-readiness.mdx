This page documents product safeguards. It does not replace the judgment
of tax, privacy, or industrial-property counsel.

## Launch status

Agata can be developed and tested without a published commercial identity.
Before accepting real payments these minima must be in place:

1. tax registration and Social Security enrollment of the party that invoices;
2. complete public legal identity;
3. an operational contact channel;
4. tax and VAT treatment validated for the countries of sale;
5. provider contracts and processing records reviewed;
6. a documented decision on health and nutrition data.

Legal pages show a readiness notice while
`LEGAL_OPERATOR_NAME`, `LEGAL_OPERATOR_TAX_ID`, or
`LEGAL_OPERATOR_ADDRESS` are missing.

## Public surfaces

| Route | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `/legal-notice` | Service provider identification, contact, and site-use rules |
| `/privacy` | GDPR information on purposes, legal bases, recipients, transfers, retention, and rights |
| `/terms` | Terms of use, plans, AI credits, cancellation, and limits |
| `/cookies` | Necessary technologies and analytics currently in use |

Legal date and version stay aligned with
`shared/constants/legal-document-versions.ts`.

## Acceptance evidence

Sign-up requires an explicit checkbox before credentials or Google. For new
accounts Better Auth stores, server-side:

- `termsAcceptedAt`;
- `termsVersion`;
- `privacyNoticeVersion`.

The migration does not backfill these fields for existing accounts. A future
re-acceptance must be explicit and keep evidence of each version; do not
invent retroactive acceptance.

## Cookies and analytics

The current setup uses essential storage for session, security, language,
theme, and UI state. Vercel Web Analytics is kept only while it operates
without cookies or persistent identifiers.

If you add advertising, pixels, replay, identifiable analytics, or any
optional storage, you must implement prior consent by category and update
`/cookies` before enabling it.

## Sensitive data

Health and nutrition may contain special categories of data. Before opening
those features to real customers you need a documented decision on purpose,
GDPR Article 9 legal basis, minimization, access, deletion, export, retention,
and incident response. If explicit consent is used, it must be demonstrable
and withdrawable without ambiguity.

The Agent must not be presented as a substitute for medical, legal, or
financial advice. Data entered in prompts may be sent to model providers and
must follow the same classification and controls.

## Providers and transfers

Keep a living inventory of every provider that processes data: purpose,
categories, region, subprocessors, contract/DPA, transfer mechanism,
retention period, and deletion procedure. The public policy must describe
real categories and practices, not names or promises that are not yet
confirmed.

## Operator changes

When moving from a sole trader to a company:

1. update the legal-identity variables;
2. review invoices, Stripe, contracts, and the bank account;
3. notify any change of controller;
4. update the documents and their version;
5. request a new acceptance when the change is material.
