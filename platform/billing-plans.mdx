Billing is based on **studio plans**, with Stripe Checkout and the customer portal when configured. `/billing` and account billing links redirect to the current studio. The plan, price, and status shown in Agata are projected from Stripe price IDs and subscription lifecycle events.

The stored free key is `base`. Product copy says **Free**. Retired Starter and Team price IDs normalize to Pro; Business is a distinct plan.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Included members | AI credits / month | Agent image pool | Studio content |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| **Free** (`base`) | $0 | 5 | 100, pooled per owner across all Free studios | 50 MB | Unlimited |
| **Pro** | $12/month or $120/year | 10 | 500 | 250 MB | Unlimited |
| **Business** | $39/month or $390/year | 25 | 2,000 | 1 GB | Unlimited |

Annual plans are billed up front. The displayed monthly equivalents are $10 for Pro and $32.50 for Business.

Tasks, projects, tags, activities, reminders, email reminder configurations, goals, finance accounts, and finance transactions do not have customer-facing plan limits. Agata keeps generous private safeguards for abuse prevention; their values and counters are not part of the product contract or usage UI.

## Recurring extras

Extras are available after activating Pro or Business. Free must upgrade first.

| Extra | Monthly | Annual | Maximum quantity per studio |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Additional member | $3/month | $30/year | 100 |
| Pack of 500 AI credits/month | $10/month | $100/year | 100 |

An annual AI pack is billed up front but grants **500 new credits each month**. Unused credits expire at the end of the monthly window and do not roll over.

`profiles.seat_count` is the effective member capacity: members included in the plan plus extra members. A reduction cannot leave capacity below active members plus pending invitations.

## AI capacity

- An accepted Agent turn consumes **1 credit**, regardless of the model or tools used.
- Free credits are pooled per owner. Paid credits stay studio-scoped and are shared by that studio's members.
- A 14-day paid trial uses the full credit pool of the selected plan.
- Metronome is the canonical ledger; Postgres keeps an operational balance projection.
- The Agent image pool is studio-wide private Blob storage under `profiles/{id}/agent/…` and follows the effective plan.
- Owners can buy credit packs from **Billing → Extras**. Other members can see the balance and must contact the owner.

## When changes apply

| Change | When it applies | If payment fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan, member, or pack increase | Immediate, with Stripe proration; capacity changes after payment confirmation | Previous plan and capacity remain active (`payment_pending`) |
| Plan downgrade, quantity reduction, or interval change | At the next renewal through a Subscription Schedule | No immediate charge |
| Mid-period AI pack increase | Proportional grant for the current window; full 500-credit grants from the next month | Same as other increases |
| Cancellation | End of period through the Stripe portal | — |
| After cancellation takes effect | Studio moves to Free | Content remains available; member, credit, and image capacity use Free entitlements |

When changing plans, extra members are recalculated to preserve total member capacity when possible. Existing studio content is never deleted or made read-only by a downgrade.

Intermediate billing states include scheduled changes (`billing_pending_*`), cancellation at period end, failed payment with a seven-day grace period, and payment confirmation pending.

## Deleting a studio with a plan

1. A blocking subscription (`active`, `trialing`, `incomplete`, `past_due` in grace, or `canceled` within its paid period) prevents soft deletion.
2. Cancel the plan in Billing or wait for it to end.
3. Move the studio to trash, which has a 30-day recovery window.
4. Permanently delete it or let the automatic purge remove it after the window.

Trash detail: [Studios, members, and follow](/concepts/organizations-studios).

## Where it is managed

| Route | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `/studios/:id/billing` | Plan status, member and credit usage, extras, invoices, and plan changes |
| `/billing` | Redirect to the current studio billing page |

The billing page is a single view with no tabs. It shows the authoritative subscription status, plan summary, member and AI credit usage, recurring extras, recent invoices, and the Free/Pro/Business comparison.

## Stripe configuration

Preview uses test Prices and Production uses live Prices. All eight `STRIPE_PRICE_*` variables, `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` are required for the billing plugin. Initial Checkout purchases the selected plan; extras are managed afterwards in Agata.

The Stripe portal owns payment methods, full invoice history, and cancellation. Opening Billing reconciles the current Stripe subscription into the Go projection when necessary. Audit records live in `plan_change_audit_log` and `billing_webhook_events`.
