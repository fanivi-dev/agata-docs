**Connections** is your account’s person-to-person network (outside a studio shell). It is not OAuth integrations with Slack, Notion, or other providers.

## What it is for

1. You invite someone by the email they use with Agata.
2. That person accepts (or declines) the request.
3. They appear on your connections list.
4. When inviting to a **studio** or adding people to an **Organization**, you can pick people from that list in one click.

## Route

```txt
/connections
```

## Capabilities

| Action | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Invite | Email → Resend + `pending` invitation. Without `RESEND_API_KEY` the API rejects the send |
| Incoming | Accept or decline |
| Outgoing | Cancel while pending |
| List | Search by name/email; remove a connection |
| Studio | “From your connections” picker when inviting members (studio selector or **Settings → Members / Invitations**) |
| Organization | Same picker when adding people from `/orgs/:orgHandle` (**Manage organization → People**). They do not automatically join Studios |

Empty search results offer **Clear search**. With no connections, the empty
state takes you to the invitation field. Incoming and outgoing invitations
use labelled keyboard-accessible tabs.

## Stack

- UI: `features/platform/domains/connections/`
- BFF: `services/connections/`
- API: `apps/api/internal/connections/` (`/users/me/connections…`)

## It is not

- External providers, third-party OAuth, or data sync from other apps.
- A substitute for studio members, **Follow**, or
  [Memberships](/platform/memberships). Collaboration lives on the studio
  team; Follow is the free audience; a subscriber is the paid audience.
  Connections only speeds inviting people you already know.
- [Explore](/platform/explore), which ranks public studios from those
  connections and from public teams you belong to.
